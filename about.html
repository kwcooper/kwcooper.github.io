Sonder (noun) — the profound feeling realization that everyone, including strangers passed on the street, has a life as complex as one's own, which they are constantly living, despite one's personal lack of awareness of it.

A good reminder of your place among the billions of people quietly living out their own complexity alongside you, on this pale blue dot.
