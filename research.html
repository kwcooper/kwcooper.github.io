[ Research ]

My work examines how learning, memory, and decision-making seamlessly integrate across brains, minds, and machines. Such interdisciplinary research spans topics in cognitive science, neuroscience, data science, and education. While my research is foundational, it is designed to inform clinical research across a wide range of conditions, such as Alzheimer's disease, aging, and addiction.
